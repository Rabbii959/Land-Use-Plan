About these figures

Areas on this dashboard are computed from the polygon geometry itself, on the WGS 84 ellipsoid, with interior rings subtracted. They are not taken from the Area_SqKm attribute in the source file, which returns zero for 11 of the 60 classes — including every urban block — and does not sum to the district's known extent. The geometric total of 2,358.9 km² falls within 0.4% of Hafizabad's gazetted area of 2,367 km², which is the check that the method is sound.

Geometry has been generalised for browser delivery using Douglas–Peucker at a tolerance of roughly 5 metres, reducing 536,288 vertices to 249,490. All 16,980 parcels are retained; areas and counts are computed from the full-resolution source before generalisation, so no figure on this page is affected by it.
