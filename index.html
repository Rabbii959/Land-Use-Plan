    About these figures

    Areas on this dashboard are computed from the polygon geometry itself, on the WGS 84 ellipsoid, with interior rings subtracted, rather than from any pre-computed area field — the current source export doesn't carry one at all. The geometric total of 2,358.9 km² falls within 0.4% of Hafizabad's gazetted area of 2,367 km², which is the check that the method is sound.

    The seven land-use classes shown as expandable groups above the map — Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Agriculture, Transportation Network, Notified Area, and Other Uses — come directly from the source layer's own LU_Class field, not an invented scheme. One data-entry typo was corrected in processing: every "Orchard" parcel carried the misspelling Agricuture in that field rather than Agriculture; it's folded into Agriculture here rather than shown as a separate class of one.

    Geometry has been generalised for browser delivery using Douglas–Peucker at a tolerance of roughly 5 metres, reducing 536,288 vertices to 249,490. All 16,980 parcels are retained; areas and counts are computed from the full-resolution source before generalisation, so no figure on this page is affected by it.

    The District and Local Govt filters draw on Punjab's own district and Local Government boundary layers, not the land-use export — every district and Local Government unit in the province is available, not just the one with parcel data loaded. Selecting a district or Local Govt zooms to and outlines its real boundary; a district other than the one with parcel data still zooms correctly, with a note that no land use parcels exist there yet rather than a silently empty map. Each Hafizabad parcel is assigned to a Local Government (a Municipal Committee or the District Council) by where its centre falls, independently of the land-use geometry — the two layers were digitised separately, so a small number of parcels near a boundary line (13 of 16,980, about 0.08%) fall outside every mapped area and are labelled accordingly rather than forced into a nearest match.
